How We Started

A name that means "Who Loves You Baby"

Kojak Limousine LLC was founded by a local St. Thomas family who saw a gap: visitors and residents alike deserved transportation that felt personal, not transactional. What began with a single vehicle and a handful of loyal clients has grown into the island's most trusted chauffeured fleet — without ever losing the warmth that started it all.

Every chauffeur we hire is background-checked, professionally trained, and — just as importantly — genuinely proud to show off their island. You'll get more than a ride. You'll get a local's welcome.
